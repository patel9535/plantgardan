Plant Care Tips

Keep your plants happy and healthy with these quick tips.

  • Water Wisely: Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Avoid overwatering.
  • Choose the Right Light: Match lighting to the plant type — some need full sun, others indirect.
  • Monitor Humidity: Mist tropical plants or use a humidifier if needed.
  • Feed Them Well: Fertilize during growing seasons (spring/summer).
  • Repot When Needed: Repot if roots outgrow the container or soil is poor.
  • Avoid Drafts: Keep plants away from AC or heater vents to reduce stress.
  • Regular Pruning: Remove dead leaves to promote healthy growth.
